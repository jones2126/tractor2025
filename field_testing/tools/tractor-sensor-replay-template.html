Evidence, calibration and interpretation limits
  • GPS path is not smoothed; a bad fix can produce a jump. Local grid projection follows the current mission replay.
  • Playback point values are the final raw row in approximately 100 ms bins. Phase, segment and health transitions preserve additional points. Gyro and acceleration ranges retain raw bin extrema; see the source CSV for full-rate analysis.
  • Host time measures read intervals, not hardware sample time. Missed IMU intervals are not reconstructed. Initial calibration has no corrected gyro/yaw.
  • Green health requires fresh packets, RTK Fixed position, valid/fixed/fresh heading within 1° accuracy, and baseline 0.80–1.30 m. This includes more than the logger’s displayed GPS=valid test.
  • GPS age here is the recorded direct-serial position decode age, not UDP delivery age or satellite measurement latency.
  • Engine events have minute precision. Engine-on is not equivalent to moving. Recorded bridge state is shown; operator engine notes remain separate from voltage and drive commands.
